Decide Together support

Decide Together helps couples, friends and families choose something together: a holiday, a film, a game, a restaurant, anything.

One person makes a deck of cards and shares a six-character code. Everyone swipes through the deck on their own phone, in private. A card becomes a match only when everyone likes it, or most people do, if the deck was set up that way. Matches go on a shared board, where you can star them, veto them, add links and mark the one you've chosen. On a holiday deck, that's called Booked.

You can start from a built-in starter deck, type or paste cards in, add photos, or have AI write cards from one sentence, such as "sunny short breaks in October". If your question isn't answered below, get in touch.

Joining a deck

With a code or a link

Ask the person who made the deck for its code. It's six characters, letters and numbers. In Decide Together, tap the join button at the top of your list of decks (a person with a plus sign), or I have a code if you don't have any decks yet. Type the code and tap Join.

If they sent you a link, tap it on your iPhone and the app opens with the code filled in. The link only works once the app is installed, so install it first, then tap the link again or type the code.

Joining any deck is free. If the app says there's no deck with that code, check it with whoever sent it.

Why can't I see anyone else's swipes?

Because nobody sees anyone else's swipes, including the person who made the deck. Your swipes are private to you, so you can say no to a card without having to explain why.

The only thing anyone learns is which cards matched. Nobody sees how you swiped on the rest.

"This deck is full"

A deck made on Free holds two people: the person who made it and one other. If you see this when you try to join, the deck already has two.

The person who made the deck can get Pro, which lets any number of people join the decks they make. Then try the same code again. You don't need Pro yourself.

Free and Pro

Joining any deck is free. Your plan decides how many decks you can make, how many people can join them, and how many AI cards you get each month.

Pro is a one-off in-app purchase, not a subscription. You pay once. The price varies by country and is shown in the app.

Who needs Pro

Only the person who makes a deck. Everyone they invite joins free, and the deck follows its maker's plan. If one person in your family has Pro, the decks they make can take everyone.

Restoring Pro on a new phone

If you've bought Pro and it isn't showing, tap Restore purchases. It's in Settings › Pro, and on the Pro screen. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.

One purchase covers the three accounts that bought or restored it most recently. That's enough for a new phone or a reinstall. If a fourth account restores it, the one that restored it longest ago goes back to Free.

Restore brings back Pro, not your decks. For those, see signing in with Apple.

Refunds

Apple takes the payment, so refunds go through Apple, not through us. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund goes through, Apple tells us and Pro ends.

Your account

Signing in with Apple

You don't have to sign in. Without it, the app uses a guest account tied to this install of the app. If you delete the app or move to a new phone, you can't get back into a guest account, or its decks.

Sign in with Apple keeps your account, so your decks are there on a new phone or after a reinstall. Apple may give us an email address, or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email. We use it only to let you back into your account, never for marketing.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings › Account › Delete account. This is what happens:

  • You leave every deck. A deck other people are in passes to whoever has been in it longest. A deck nobody else is in is deleted, with its photos.
  • Cards and links you added to shared decks stay, without your name. Photos you added are removed.
  • Your swipes, votes, display name and account are deleted.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the app's access.
  • Your Pro purchase isn't lost. It belongs to your Apple ID, so Restore purchases brings it back on a new account.

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Use Delete account first, or contact us. The privacy policy has the full detail.

AI cards

What's sent

Describe it (on a holiday deck, Or describe the trip) writes cards from one sentence, such as "sunny short breaks in October". When you use it, our server sends that sentence to Anthropic's Claude, which writes the cards. With it go the deck's type and title, the titles of cards already in the deck so it doesn't repeat them, and anything you wrote under "Anything we should know?". For a holiday deck, it also sends the destinations with direct flights from your home airports.

Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you use the feature, and its terms don't let it train its models on what we send. Our server then looks for freely licensed photos on Wikimedia Commons and Openverse, by card title. The privacy policy has the detail.

The monthly allowance

Free includes 24 AI cards a month and Pro includes 120. The allowance resets at the start of each month, and requests that fail don't count.

AI cards come out of the allowance of whoever asks for them, on their own plan, whoever's deck they're adding to.
