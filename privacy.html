Privacy policy

Sorted keeps what it needs to run shared decks. Your swipes are never shown to anyone else. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Sorted is made by Jonny Stokes, an independent developer in the UK, who is the data controller: the person responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law. "We" and "us" on this page mean Jonny.

You can reach Jonny at [contact email to be added].

Your account

The app makes a guest account for you automatically. It is a random account ID, with no email address or phone number. You choose a display name, and the people in your decks see it.

If you want to keep your account on another device, you can Sign in with Apple. This is optional. Apple then gives us a stable identifier for your Apple ID, and may give us an email address. If you choose Hide My Email, that is a private relay address that forwards to you. We use these only to let you back into your account, never for marketing.

What we store

On our server

Our server runs on Supabase, a hosted database, storage and sign-in service. Supabase is our processor: it holds and handles the data for us, on our instructions. It may process data outside the UK, under its data processing terms. We store:

  • the decks you make or join, with their settings (such as home airports and anything written under "Anything we should know?"), and their members' display names;
  • the cards, with their titles, descriptions, any photos you add, and the sentence an AI deck was made from;
  • your swipes. These are private to you: other members only ever learn which cards matched;
  • your stars and vetoes on the board, which the deck's members can see;
  • links you add;
  • a record of each AI request: when it was made, how many cards, which model, its size in tokens, and whether it worked;
  • your purchase, if you buy Pro (see purchases).

Our server functions keep short-lived logs, with your account ID, what was asked for and any errors, so we can fix problems.

Photos you add

A photo you add to a card is stored so everyone in the deck can see it. It sits at a public web address that can't be guessed. Anyone given that exact address could open the photo, whether or not they are in the deck.

Other services, and when they are involved

AI cards

"Describe it" (on a holiday deck, "Or describe the trip") has AI write cards from a sentence you type. When you use it, our server sends Anthropic, through its Claude API:

  • the sentence you typed;
  • the deck's type, and its title if you have given it one;
  • anything you wrote under "Anything we should know?";
  • the titles of cards already in the deck, so it doesn't repeat them;
  • for a holiday deck, the destinations with direct flights from your home airports.

Anthropic writes the cards and sends them back to our server. It processes the request under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on the request. See Anthropic's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you use the feature.

Pictures for AI cards

Every card written by AI gets a picture. For holidays, places to eat and other real things, our server first searches Wikimedia Commons and Openverse by card title for freely licensed photos. The app shows each photo's photographer and licence.

Films, games, and cards with no suitable photo get an illustration instead. Our server sends fal.ai a short description of a scene for that card, such as "a pink hotel on a snowy mountainside", and fal's image model paints it. Claude writes the description about the card itself: it is not what you typed, and Claude is told to leave out anything from "Anything we should know?". The app labels these pictures as AI-generated. We keep them in our storage and reuse them for the same card in other people's decks.

Your phone loads photos directly from the sites that host them, and illustrations from our storage. Openverse finds photos hosted on other sites, so a photo found through it loads from that site. Each site sees your phone's IP address, as it would for any website visit. Photos on the built-in holiday cards come with the app and load from nowhere.

Links you add

When you add a link to a card on the board, your phone fetches that page over https to show its title. The site sees your IP address, as it would for any website visit.

Booking links

On a holiday card marked Booked, "Find flights" and "Find hotels" open Skyscanner, Google Flights or Booking.com in your browser. Their own privacy policies apply there.

If a link carries our affiliate code, the site knows the visit came from us and may pay us a commission. We don't receive your personal details from them.

Purchases

Apple takes the payment for Pro. We never see your card or billing details.

Apple sends us a signed record of the purchase: a transaction number, the product, the dates, whether it has been refunded, and whether it was a test or a real purchase. We link it to your account so Pro works and Restore purchases can bring it back. Apple also sends us notice of refunds, so Pro ends after one.

Who handles your data

Every service involved in running Sorted, in one place:

Supabase
Our database, file storage, sign-in and server functions. Everything under what we store is held here.
Anthropic
Writes AI cards, only when you use "Describe it".
Apple
Takes payments for Pro, and runs Sign in with Apple if you use it.
Wikimedia Commons and Openverse
Photo search for AI cards, by card title.
fal.ai
Paints illustrations for AI cards from a description of the card. Nothing about you is sent.

What we don't do

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling your data, and no sharing it for advertising.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete the deck it belongs to, or your account. Guest accounts that aren't in any decks may be removed after 30 days.

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Use Delete account in the app first, or contact us.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account. Then:

  • You leave every deck. A deck other people are in passes to the person who has been in it longest. A deck nobody else is in is deleted, with its photos.
  • Cards and links you added to shared decks stay, without your name. Photos you added are removed.
  • Your swipes, votes, display name and account are deleted.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the app's access.

Two records stay:

  • Your purchase record, without your account ID, because the purchase belongs to your Apple ID. You don't need to buy again: Restore purchases brings Pro back on a new account.
  • The record of AI requests, without your account ID, for our monthly cost totals.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also ask us to restrict how we use it, object to how we use it, and have a copy in a form you can take elsewhere (portability).

To use any of these rights, contact us at [contact email to be added]. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
